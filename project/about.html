About Taskly

Taskly is a simple productivity website designed to help people organize their daily activities, stay focused, and build better habits through task management.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide an easy-to-use tool that helps users stay organized without unnecessary complexity. Taskly focuses on simplicity, productivity, and accessibility for everyone.

Who Can Use Taskly?

Taskly is perfect for students, professionals, freelancers, or anyone who wants to manage daily responsibilities more efficiently.

How It Works

1. Create

Add your tasks with a category, priority, and due date.

2. Organize

Use filters and search to quickly find what you need.

3. Complete

Mark tasks as completed and monitor your progress through the dashboard.

Main Features

  • Create, edit, and delete tasks.
  • Search tasks instantly.
  • Filter by category or status.
  • Track your progress with live statistics.
  • Automatically save your information using localStorage.
